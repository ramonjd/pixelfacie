The EU AI Act is Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. It entered into force on 1 August 2024.[^in-force]

It sorts AI systems by how much harm they could do, from banned practices[^banned] down to systems with no new duties.

## What I want to find out

- Where the line between the risk levels falls.
- What the Act asks of the big general-purpose models.
- Who it applies to outside the EU.
